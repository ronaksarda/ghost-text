Notes from a slow October

October 20, 2026 · 4 min read

The sun sets at 6:12 now. I noticed because the light leaves my desk before I leave my work, and for the first time in years that felt like a signal rather than a nuisance.

Finishing is a skill

Starting is cheap. I have a folder of forty half-built tools to prove it. This month I gave myself one rule: nothing new until the oldest unfinished thing is either shipped or deleted.

I deleted eleven of them. Shipping the twelfth took three evenings, and nobody noticed, which turned out to be fine.
